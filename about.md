## 这一站是做什么的

作曲家站是**人物档案**。它以人为线索，把作品、时代与风格串起来 ——
因为很多音乐问题，换个角度从「谁写的、写给谁、为什么这么写」入手，一下就通了。

## 收录哪些作曲家

- **作曲家档案** —— 生平与时代（一生大致怎么走、当时是什么样的音乐环境）、
  风格特征（听得出来的特点）、与同时代人的关系。
- **代表作品** —— 每位挑出若干首，每首一句「听点」：听什么、注意哪里。
- **可播实例** —— 档案里提到的曲目都会给出库里真实存在的版本，点一下就能听。
- **中国作曲家** —— 作品版权状态清楚、可以公开引用与播放的中国作曲家在此收录。

## 每条长什么样

- 先立**一个身份**：一句话说明他为什么值得记住。
- 再给**一条时间线**：出生、师承、任职、重要作品、晚年。
- 然后**几首代表作**，每首一句话说明该听什么。
- 中英双语，人名与生卒年等事实用同一口径书写。

## 怎么读

1. **当工具书查** —— 听到一首不熟悉的曲子，先看是谁写的。
2. **当入口用** —— 从一位作曲家出发，走到体裁、乐器与时期。
3. **当听单用** —— 只看「代表作品」，一路听下去。

## 档案什么时候来

档案正在陆续上线。这一站依赖**身份的逐条核对** —— 同名、异体拼写、生卒年、
作品归属都要核过才敢写，所以会**一批一批地发**，先把最容易混淆的那批人做扎实。
中国作曲家的收录标准（年代与版权状态）也会先写明，再开写。

## What this station is for

This is the **people** station. It follows the person as the thread that ties works, era
and style together — because many musical questions open right up once you approach them
from "who wrote this, for whom, and why in this way".

## Whose profiles are included

- **Composer profiles** — life and era (roughly how the life went, and what the musical
  world around it was like), stylistic traits you can actually hear, and relationships with
  contemporaries.
- **Key works** — a handful per composer, each with one line on what to listen for.
- **Playable examples** — every work mentioned gets a version that really exists in the
  library, one click away.
- **Chinese composers** — included where the copyright status is clear enough to quote and
  play the music publicly.

## What each profile looks like

- It opens with **an identity**: one line on why this person is worth remembering.
- Then **a timeline**: birth, teachers, posts, major works, later years.
- Then **a few key works**, each with one line on what to listen for.
- In both Chinese and English, with names and dates written consistently in each.

## How to read it

1. **As a reference** — hear something unfamiliar and want to know who wrote it.
2. **As a gateway** — start from a composer and move out to genres, instruments and periods.
3. **As a playlist** — read only the key works and listen straight through.

## When the profiles arrive

Profiles are being added over time. This station depends on **checking identities one by
one** — shared names, variant spellings, dates and attributions all have to be verified
before anything is written — so it is being published **in batches**, starting with the
people who are easiest to confuse. The inclusion criteria for Chinese composers (dates and
copyright status) will be stated before the writing starts.
